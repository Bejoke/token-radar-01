How the model works

One function scores every protocol. Nothing is hard-coded by name: the same four multipliers price every row, and the category multiples are computed from this snapshot's own live-token protocols.

  1. Category multiple — the median market-cap-over-TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category (at least 3 peers, else the median over all live-token protocols), clamped to 0.05 – 1.00.
  2. Growth — the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50% … +100%, at a weight of 0.6: multiplier 1 + 0.6 × growth.
  3. Maturity — 0.60 + 0.40 × min(age, 1095 days) / 1095. A protocol that has run longer with no token is worth more; an unknown listing date counts as 365 days.
  4. Chains — 1 + 0.02 per extra chain, up to 5 extra chains.

implied value = TVL × category × growth × maturity × chains, rounded to whole dollars.

What the board leaves out

Bridges, canonical bridges, liquid staking, liquid restaking, restaked BTC and CEX protocols are out: they are wrapped or staked products of a token that already trades, not launches of their own. So are versioned pools (a name ending in V2, v2 or 2.0), names carrying the word wrapped, staked or staking, and protocols named after an exchange. The snapshot is sealed at its fetch date and never moves; the board is a reading of it, not a prediction of whether or when any token is issued.
